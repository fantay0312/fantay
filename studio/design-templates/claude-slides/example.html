Slide composition guide

Designing slides for the back of the room

Margins, type and vertical space on a 1920 by 1080 canvas.

Three habits

What good decks share

01

Outline first

Write every title before any slide. Read only the titles: the story should already make sense.

02

One idea per slide

A statement beats bullets. Lists become tables, card rows, big numbers or quotes.

03

A fixed system

One type scale, one margin, one place for the heading, repeated on every slide.

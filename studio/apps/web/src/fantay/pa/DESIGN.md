# Fantay workspace (formerly PA)

One-page guide for maintainers. The redesign spec (2026-11-10) and the Soul specs (companion chat, 2026-11) are the source of these rules; this page keeps the parts every change must respect.

## Binding rules

- **Owner taste.** Every tab is inline (never a "更多" overflow; a per-row ⋯ action menu is fine). Creating, editing and settings open a Fantay `Modal`; nothing unfolds inline and pushes the page (a `Popover` only for one value). Segmented and filter rows span the full row with equal segments; KPI and footer text never truncates. Settings-like pages and modals are a heading plus hairline group blocks. Disclosures are `PaDisclosure` chevron rows, never a raw `<details>`. Chat first: no type or mode question before the owner says anything. Desktop only, light and dark, Chinese first (English must fit too).
- **Governance** (`tests/fantay/governance.test.ts`). No raw `<button|select|textarea|input>` (a hidden `type="file"` input excepted); use `../ui`. No `text-[Npx]`, `rounded-[Npx]`, bold weights or hex colours in `.tsx`. SPDX `AGPL-3.0-only` header on every source. Rule B1: a PA module may read only tokens from `packages/fantay-tokens/tokens.css`, `styles/legacy-aliases.css`, CSS under `src/fantay/` or inline styles — never `--chat-*`, `--font-size-*`, `--radius-xl`, `--shadow-md`, `--ease-out`. PA tokens live in `src/fantay/styles/pa.css`. Copy goes through `t('pa.…')` from `src/i18n/pa-ui/*.ts` (Soul units `soul-*.ts`): a key lands in `…En`, `…Zh` and `…Ja` together (each dictionary is typed to carry every key). Soul runtime code comes from `@open-design/contracts/soul`.
- **Honesty.** Activity comes from persisted events and goal progress comes from linked tasks. Routines run only at opted-in daily slots while the local service is available. Voice records on click and returns an editable draft. Never offer Resume when the daemon would refuse it (`pa-model.canResume`). Unreported usage reads "用量未报告", never 0. Channel chats say desktop replies do not go back to the channel.
- **Honesty in a companion chat.** Every indicator derives from stored or streamed state. 正在输入… and the typing bubble show only while `typingAgents(index, conversationId)` is non-empty (the active chat task's running attempt or next participant); nothing is synthesised as a message. Live text in the typing row is the running attempt's real partial output (the daemon's `liveReplyText`, polled from `GET /api/pa/soul/live`); the stored reply replaces it without a second reveal. A stopped or failed partial reply is never stored: it stays as a labelled draft for the session and is gone after the next turn, a dismiss or a conversation switch. Speech is generated only on 朗读, under the chat's 自动朗读 setting (default 点了才读), or on 试听 in the profile editor (one sample line with the voice as picked, saved or not; the row says it calls the speech service), and a voice bar exists only for a file that was really generated: no fake waveform, no browser speech synthesis. The paced bubble reveal (`useSoulReveal`, the engine's `revealPlan`) is presentation only: the composer and the typing state never wait for it, and a key, a click or scrolling up shows the rest. No online dot, read marks, receipts, counters or durations, and no task, model, queue, step or token words in the companion roster row, header, transcript, composer or inspector (token numbers only in the 上下文 modal, the profile editor's estimates and the lorebook editor). Dice, draws and events are stored story messages captioned 故事: never editable, but hideable (不让 TA 看到) and deletable, refused with 409 while a chat turn of that conversation is queued or running. A technical failure reads as the app's failure, never as the character declining. The tray chip 让 TA 先开口 appears after a long quiet (`needsReachoutRow`) and has no scheduler behind it; the scheduled 先开口 is a routine set in 对话设置 (direct companion chats only) that runs only at its time while Fantay is open, never 23:00–8:00, at most twice a day, and pauses after three unanswered or for 24 h after a care case (daemon `reachoutBlocked`).
- **`.fx` preflight.** The view renders in `FxRoot`; upstream markup (`renderMarkdown`, `.prose-block`, `ChatRoot`) goes inside `FxLegacy`.

## Layout

- `PersonalAgentWorkspace.tsx` is the root: `FxRoot[data-testid=pa-workspace]`, the roster (`--pa-roster-col`) beside the main area. The main area is a conversation (header, transcript, tray and state strip, composer; inspector beside it) or one page that replaces the whole chat area, inspector included.
- **Roster** (`PaRoster`): search with ＋ (`SoulRosterMenu`: new character, import a card, new group chat, starters), the full-width 全部 · 聊天 · 工作 filter, the pinned Fantay row, then one list of characters, group chats and channel chats. Under the list the four-cell bottom bar (`SoulBottomBar`) 角色库 · 记忆 · 工作 · 连接, with the needs-you count on 工作 and a warm dot on 连接; the rail draws the same four places as icons.
- **Pages.** 角色库 (`soul/SoulLibraryPage`: 角色 · 世界书 · 我的人设 · 文风; 角色 holds the tag filter, the 入门 shelf in the grid's columns and 我的角色) · 记忆 (`PaMemoryPage`) · 工作 (one `SoulWorkTabs` strip: 任务 is the `tasks` place; 动态 · 目标 · 例行 are the `activity` place's panes; a 先开口 routine is tagged and edited in its 对话设置) · 连接 (`PaConnectionsPage`).
- **Mode.** `conversationMode(index, conversation)` decides companion or work and is never asked up front: an explicit choice wins, a channel chat is work unless its channel's 聊天模式 is on, otherwise a chat is companion when everyone in it has a character card. It is switched from the header ⋯ menu or 对话设置, which offer it only where a companion chat is possible (not Fantay, not a channel). A Feishu or WeChat channel has its own 聊天模式 switch in the channel modal, offered when the channel's target is a character or a group chat of characters: plain messages are then chat turns answered with the reply itself (no receipt, no task id; `/task` still runs a task), private memory is neither read nor proposed there, and a care case sends the care line after the reply. In enterprise mode (`index.enterprise`) the library is shared and written only by members with `index.canManageLibrary` (admins, or a member granted the library): everyone else reads it, and `nav.openModal` refuses the library modals (`agent`, `group`, `cardImport`, `lorebook`, `preset`) with one toast while the create, import, edit and starter affordances are hidden. Conversations, personas, chat settings and what a character remembers belong to one member each: only a conversation with the member's `principalId` can be a companion chat, a conversation without one is a shared work room, and opening a character creates the member's own chat. 先开口 is not offered there (no routines). When the daemon follows an organization server (`snapshot.org`), the library is read-only for everyone, the library page says when it was last synced (or why the last sync failed), and admins get 立即同步. In companion mode the chat column carries `soulThemeAttrs(…)` and `data-mode="companion"`, so the theme paper covers header, transcript and the composer's surroundings; work mode carries `data-mode="work"` and no theme, so it looks as before.
- **Per-turn rendering.** `PaTranscript` hands off to `soul/SoulTranscript` when the chat is companion or holds any message with `message.soul`. It walks `soulTurns`: chat turns are bubbles, work turns are `PaMessage` + `PaTurnTail` (boxed with a task chip inside a companion transcript). Otherwise the work path is untouched.
- **Inspector.** Work: 动态 · 确认 · 档案 (`PaInspector`). Companion: the same panel and head over `soul/SoulInspector` with TA · 此刻 · 记忆, closed by default. The open state is remembered per mode (`inspectorOpen`, `inspectorOpenCompanion`); `usePa().ui.inspectorOpen` is already resolved for the open chat.
- **Modals** (`ui.modal`, one at a time through `PaModalHost`): `agent` (profile editor: eleven sections for a character, 立绘 after 外观; three for Fantay), `group`, `memory`, `task`, `amend`, `reconcile`, `limits`, `channel`, `cardImport`, `lorebook`, `persona`, `preset`, `appearance`, `chatSettings` (对话设置, including 先开口), `context` (上下文: what a reply saw, read-only).
- `src/fantay/styles/pa.css` makes the PA view full-bleed inside the entry shell (`entry-view-pa[data-active=true]`).
- `usePaLayout` measures the host (1280 until measured; a hidden view keeps its last width):

| Host width | Roster | Inspector |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 980 | full 260 | docked, 340 (300–420, chat keeps ≥ 420) |
| 900–979 | full 260 | floating 340; does not change the docked preference |
| 720–899 | rail 64 (full list in a left `Sheet`) | floating 340 |
| < 720 | left `Sheet`, 300 | floating, `min(340, W − 24)` |

- Only these scroll: the roster list, the transcript, the inspector pane body, the page body, a modal body, the `@` list. The composer never scrolls away.
- View state (`place`, conversation, inspector, filters, library tab and query, modal, roster query) lives in the root and survives tab switches (the view stays mounted). Per-viewer `localStorage`, each guarded with try/catch: `fantay.pa.ui.v1` `{conversationId, inspectorOpen, inspectorOpenCompanion, inspectorWidth}`, `fantay.pa.seen.v1` the unread map, `fantay.pa.library.shelf.v1` the 入门 shelf's 收起, `fantay.pa.care.dismissed.v1` dismissed care cards.
- Default conversation: the remembered one, else Fantay's latest direct chat, else the first group chat, else the first conversation, else the Fantay empty state.

## Mascot and avatars

- **Fantay** is the main assistant: the agent with `avatar === 'fantay'`, else `agents[0]` (`pa-model.findFantay`). Its displayed name is always the live `agent.name`; the module label is the static `pa.title` = "Fantay". Only Fantay wears the mascot; a clone loses it.
- **Live mascot budget: at most 3 on screen** — the sidebar/rail icon while work runs (dwell 600 ms), the inspector hero (104, when open), and one transcript slot (the empty-state greeting when the inspector is closed, or the working line; never both). Everywhere else use the zero-JS `FantayMascotStill` (rail/roster disc 34, rows 28, clusters 65 %, empty illustrations 48, receipts 20).
- **Other agents** (`PaAvatar`): five folded-paper faces, stable per agent ID, tinted sage, teal, ochre, rose or slate. The mascot keeps its own silhouette and palette. The owner message bubble uses charcoal with light ink in dark mode.
- **Group clusters** (`PaGroupAvatar`): 1 face; 2 on a diagonal at 65 %; 3 in a triangle at 54 %; 4+ two faces plus "+N". Each face on a 2 px `--paper` collar; Fantay on a surface disc.
- **Characters** (`soul/SoulPortrait`): never a live mascot. A portrait image is cover-fit on a top focal point; without one, rows and headers keep the `PaAvatar` face, and covers (library tiles, 入门 cards; starters ship no art) draw a monogram on the character's theme paper. Fantay's library tile is `FantayMascotStill` 64 on `--pa-fantay-disc`. A portrait (and a persona avatar) may be cropped 1:1 on upload (`media-tools` `CropDialog`). Sprites (`character.sprites`, one image per emotion) follow the stored mood of the character's newest visible reply in its own direct chat while that reply is younger than 24 h (`emotionOfReply`): the header portrait, the inspector hero and, when the appearance turns on 立绘, the standing picture beside a wide transcript; a mood without its own image uses 平静, then the portrait.
- **Colour meaning.** Warm (`--warning`) only for needs you (waiting tasks that need an action, channel approvals, uncertain deliveries). Failed is `--accent-ink`. Other colour lives only in the transcript theme, portraits and covers, the five avatar hues, the mascot and the send disc. Everything else is ink.

## Soul theme

- Tokens in `src/fantay/styles/pa.css`: `--pa-soul-*` are mode-dependent chrome (portrait ring, tags, state bars, hover on a paper, the drop veil, pattern alphas); `--pa-skin-*` are the transcript theme, one light and one dark block for each of the seven themes (fantay 默认, clean, sakura, archive, rain, ink, deepsea), with the default skin also on `:root`. Bubble shape, font and size are separate attributes (`data-pa-shape`, `data-pa-font`, `data-pa-scale`).
- `soul/soul-theme.ts` (`soulThemeAttrs`) turns a resolved appearance into `data-pa-skin`, `data-pa-pattern`, `data-pa-frame`, `data-pa-layout` (`bubble` or `journal`, the 手帐 layout with a name · time head line per cluster) and those three, plus `data-pa-accent` when a custom accent is set; `soul/SoulThemeScope` draws a themed island (preview, swatches). Transcript CSS reads `--pa-skin-*` only; chrome reads base tokens.
- A custom accent is drawn through the engine's `guardAccent`, which keeps it as picked or moves its lightness separately for light and dark until it reads on the theme's paper, so an unreadable value is never drawn. The polaroid frame (`avatarFrame: 'polaroid'`) is a white-bordered square portrait.

## Motion

Tokens (`src/fantay/styles/pa.css`): `--pa-ease` quint `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`, `--pa-ease-fold` `cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1)` (folds only), `--pa-ease-pop` (only the step-done tick), `--pa-dur-quick 120`, `--pa-dur-enter 240`, `--pa-dur-exit 160`, `--pa-dur-fold 280`, `--pa-dur-swap 300`, `--pa-loop-shimmer 1.8s`, `--pa-loop-typing 1.2s`.

| Moment | Motion |
|---|---|
| New turn or message | opacity + `translateY(6px)`, 240 ms (history renders still) |
| Conversation switch | fade in 200 ms, no slide, no exit |
| Working line | presence 160 ms; enter opacity + 4 px, 200 ms; label `SwapLabel` 300 ms; shimmer 1.8 s linear |
| Needs-you / approval card, story card | opacity + `scale(.97 → 1)`, 240 ms; answered card dims to .7 over 160 ms |
| Typing bubble | in opacity + 4 px, 240 ms; dots wave at `--pa-loop-typing`; out 160 ms as the first reply bubble takes its place |
| Paced reveal | each bubble rises (opacity + 6 px, 240 ms) at the offsets of `revealPlan` |
| Theme apply | paper and bubble colours over 240 ms |
| Docked inspector | column width 0 ↔ W, 240 ms open / 160 ms close; content keeps its width and fades |
| Floating inspector | opacity + `translateX(12px)`, 200 ms in / 150 ms out |
| Inspector tabs | chip glides 240 ms; pane items enter ±8 px, staggered 18 ms over 6 |
| Disclosure | grid rows 0fr ↔ 1fr + opacity, 280 ms fold / 160 ms close; chevron 90° in 150 ms |
| Row hover | background 120 ms; selection instant; hover actions swap in place ±5 px over 160 ms |
| Modals, sheet, popovers | the primitives' own motion (`fx-modal-in` from .96) |

Never animate from `scale(0)` (surfaces ≥ .96, icons ≥ .6). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` only opacity changes of ≤ 160 ms remain: no shimmer, stagger or glide, the mascot is static, typing dots hold at .6 and the paced reveal shows every bubble at once.

## Lab

`/fantay-ui/pa-lab` (`lab/PaLab.tsx`) renders the production workspace over `lab/fixture-transport.ts` with the fixtures of `lab/fixtures.ts` and `lab/soul-fixtures.ts`. Controls mirror `?scenario=` (`first-run`, `fantay-chat`, `needs-you`, `project-done`, `group-three`, `inspector`, `connections`, `offline`, `long-cjk`, `first-run-soul`, `companion-chat`, `story-game`, `companion-group`, `library`, `live-chat`, `voice-chat`, `sprites-chat`, `journal-chat`), `&theme=light|dark`, `&locale=zh-CN|en`, `&width=1192|1032|860|700`, `&tab=<inspector tab>`. Knobs without a control: `&latency=<ms>`, `&stale=1`, `&stream=0` (no live text, dots only), `&hold=1` (the live text stops part way and the turn stays running), `&speech=none` (no speech service), and the appearance of the open chat: `&skin=<theme>`, `&layout=bubble|journal`, `&frame=circle|squircle|polaroid`, `&pattern=none|grid|ruled|dots`, `&stage=side|off`, `&accent=<rrggbb>`. Deep links read once for screenshots: `&inspector=1` (open in both modes), `&place=<place>`, `&ltab=<library tab>`, `&conv=<conversationId>`, `&modal=<URL-encoded JSON PaModalState>`.

## Where things live

`pa-types.ts` (contracts and view state) · `pa-model.ts` (pure rules mirroring `apps/daemon/src/services/personal-agent-service.ts`) · `soul/soul-model.ts` (pure adapters to the engine `@open-design/contracts/soul`, source `packages/contracts/src/soul/`: mode, variants, context and the state fold are never re-implemented) · `soul/soul-commands.ts` (slash commands and the ＋ menu) · `soul/soul-theme.ts` · `soul/soul-files.ts` (card, CHARX and world-book files; CHARX export packs the saved portrait and sprites with the engine's `buildCharx`) · `soul/soul-live.ts` (live text: a 250 ms poll of `GET /api/pa/soul/live` only while a chat turn of the open chat runs, and the session-only draft) · `soul/soul-voice.ts` (speech status, 朗读, 试听 and the one audio player) · `usePaWorkspace.ts` (polling while active: snapshot 2 s with 2→4→8 s backoff, 700 ms while a chat turn is queued or running, the soul snapshot refetched when `soulVersion` moves, control and channels 5 s, models per activation; every action and its pending key) · `pa-transport.tsx` (injectable request function for the lab and tests) · shell: `PersonalAgentWorkspace`, `PaRoster`, `soul/SoulBottomBar`, `PaModalHost` · conversation: `PaConversationView`, `PaChatHeader`, `PaTranscript` / `soul/SoulTranscript`, `soul/SoulTray`, `PaComposer` · other Soul pieces, pages and modals in `soul/` · copy in `src/i18n/pa-ui/` · tests in `apps/web/tests/fantay/pa/` · daemon `apps/daemon/src/services/personal-agent-soul.ts` with `/api/pa/soul/*` in `routes/personal-agent-soul.ts`; the in-memory live buffer `services/personal-agent-soul-live.ts` (route `routes/personal-agent-soul-live.ts`) and the speech port `services/personal-agent-soul-speech.ts`: the signed-in TokenToSea account's speech models first (each model's verified catalogue operation, billed by characters to the TokenToSea balance), then the media speech providers set up in Settings. `PaSpeechStatus.default` is the voice of a character that has none of its own (TokenToSea's default speech model), so read-aloud works once signed in; the profile editor shows it as 默认 · {service} and any other service or voice can be picked instead. Stored files nothing refers to any more (an image uploaded for a form that was never saved, a replaced portrait or sprite, audio of a deleted message, a voice sample nobody replayed for a week) are removed by `PaSoulService.sweepFiles`, run from the service's maintenance tick at most every six hours; an image younger than a day always stays.
